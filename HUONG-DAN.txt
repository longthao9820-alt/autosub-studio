AUTOSUB STUDIO 2.0.0  -  BAN CHAY DOC LAP
=========================================

Thu muc nay da co san moi thu: FFmpeg, mo hinh doc chu tren hinh
va thu vien tang toc bang card do hoa. Mo hinh giong noi ASR (Whisper)
se duoc tai tu dong ve Data\models\asr khi dung lan dau (giu bo cai <2 GB)
hoac tu dong chuyen tu ban V1 cu sang.
KHONG can cai dat gi them.


TANG TOC BANG CARD DO HOA
-------------------------
Phan mem TU DONG dung card do hoa cua may, khong phai bat gi.

So lieu tham khao truoc day tren card NVIDIA RTX 3060:
   Nhan dang giong noi   : nhanh gap 3,6 lan CPU
   Doc chu tren hinh OCR : nhanh gap 4,7 lan CPU
   Render video          : nhanh gap 4,0 lan CPU

May co card NVIDIA  -> nhanh ca ba viec
May co chip Intel   -> render nhanh (Quick Sync)
May co card AMD     -> render nhanh (AMF)
May khong co card   -> van chay binh thuong bang CPU, chi cham hon

Truoc khi giao viec cho card, phan mem chay thu mot tam anh nho. May nao
thieu thu vien thi no tu doc bang CPU, khong bao loi giua chung.

Dieu kien duy nhat: may do da cai driver card do hoa (may dung binh
thuong thi da co san). Khong phai cai CUDA Toolkit.

Muon xem may dang dung gi: nhap dup KIEM-TRA-MAY-NAY.bat,
hoac xem dong trang thai o tab "Cai dat chung".

Muon tat tang toc: tab "Cai dat chung", bo chon hai o
"Nhan dang giong noi bang card do hoa" va "Render video bang phan cung".


MO PHAN MEM
-----------
Nhap dup vao tep  AutoSubStudio.exe

Lan dau Windows co the hien bang "Windows protected your PC".
Bam More info -> Run anyway.

Muon mo nhanh: nhap chuot phai vao AutoSubStudio.exe ->
Send to -> Desktop (create shortcut).


CHUYEN SANG MAY KHAC
--------------------

hay mang deu duoc), roi nhap dup AutoSubStudio.exe la chay ngay.

Du an va cai dat cua ban nam trong thu muc "Data" ben trong, nen chep
di dau cung khong mat.

Muon chac chan may moi chay duoc:
  - Nhap dup  KIEM-TRA-MAY-NAY.bat   (kiem tra nhanh, khoang 10 giay)
  - Nhap dup  KIEM-TRA-SAU.bat       (chay thu that, khoang 1-2 phut)
Ket qua hien len man hinh va luu vao tep ket-qua-kiem-tra.txt


LAM MOT VIDEO
-------------
1. Tab "Danh sach du an"   -> Tao du an moi -> chon video
2. Tab "B1: Tach sub"      -> Lay phu de bang giong noi
                              (chay ngay, khong can Internet)

Neu video da co phu de tieng Trung chay san tren hinh:
  - Chon "Tach Bang Chu (OCR)"
  - De Ngon Ngu Sub = Simplified Chinese
  - De Che Do Trich Xuat = Chinh Xac
  - De Server = PP-OCRv6 Medium (Chuan nhat)
  - Khoanh sat vung phu de, khong lay qua nhieu logo/chu quang cao

Che do Chinh Xac lay 6 hinh/giay, bo ket qua duoi 50%, doc lai ca dong
va doi chieu 3 khung lien tiep. Mo hinh da duoc dong goi san, khong can mang.
   Hoac neu video da co chu chay san tren hinh (hardsub):
      a) Mo du an -> video hien hinh ngay, va khung xanh CO SAN o phan
         duoi man hinh Screen Edit. Khong phai bat tat gi ca.
      b) Keo GIUA khung de di chuyen den cho co chu
         Keo 8 NUT XANH o vien de phong to / thu nho khung
         Luc dang keo, phan ngoai khung mo di cho de ngam
      c) Bam "Lay phu de bang OCR"
      Muon dua khung ve cho ban dau: bam "Dat Lai Vung Mac Dinh"
      Khung xanh CHI co o Screen Edit. Tab Screen Render de xem lai
      thanh pham nen khong co khung nao che tam nhin.

   CAU HINH MAC DINH GIONG NTS
   Anh goc duoc dua thang vao PP-OCRv4, khong tu loc theo mau va khong
   tu bo chu dung yen. Video thong thuong chi can khoanh sat dong phu de.
     - Loc MAU CHU la tuy chon chi dinh. De trong = KHONG LOC MAU.
       Chi bam "Chon Mau..." hoac nhap #RRGGBB khi trong vung OCR co
       logo/chu quang cao khac mau. Bam "Clear" de tat va dung anh goc.
       Video net de sai lech 10-15%, video mo thi co the tang len.
     - Theo CHIEU CAO CHU: o "Loai Bo Chieu Cao Chu: Nho Hon < ... Va
       Lon Hon > ..." bo cac dong qua thap (chu ghi chu nho) hoac qua
       cao (chu tieu de to). Mac dinh 25-200 px giong du an NTS cua ban.
       Nen khoanh sat phu de; bam "Do Chieu Cao Chu" neu muon do tu video.
   Video qua toi hoac qua bac lam chu chim vao nen: keo them hai thanh
   "Chinh Sang" va "Chinh Tuong Phan".
    Ket qua do tham khao truoc day tren mot video dung san (nen nhieu mau, nen manh):
    truoc khi loc doc ra 12 cau, giong ban goc 77%; sau khi loc con dung
    6 cau, giong ban goc 99,5%.

    MOC THOI GIAN DUOC DO HAI LAN CHO KHOP
    Lan dau quet thua nen moc chi dung trong khoang nua giay. Sau do
    phan mem lay them cac khung sat nhau quanh dau va cuoi moi cau de
    tim dung luc chu hien ra va bien mat (do thu truoc day giup sai so
    trung binh giam ro ret).
    Doi lai thoi gian chay lau hon.
    Muon nhanh hon: tab B1 bo chon "Do lai moc thoi gian cho chinh xac"

   TACH SUB XONG LA CO NGAY TEP .SRT
   Tep phu de duoc tu dong luu vao DUNG THU MUC chua video ban da them,
   trung ten voi video:  tap 01.mp4  ->  tap 01.srt
   Khong phai bam xuat tay. Lan tach sau ghi de dung tep do.
   Neu canh video da co san tep .srt trung ten tu truoc thi phan mem
   de yen tep cu va luu thanh tap 01_1.srt.
   Muon tat: tab "Cai dat chung" -> bo chon
   "Tach sub xong thi luu luon tep .srt canh video"
3. Sua lai phu de o bang goc tren ben phai.
   Bam "Kiem tra time" de do cac cau bi loi thoi gian.
4. Tab "B2: Dich noi dung" -> chon ngon ngu -> Dich toan bo du an
                              (dung Google Dich hoac AI Gateway)
5. Tab "B3: Ghep Giong Doc"
   - Chon giong doc tu danh muc Local Voice (Piper Local). Neu model
     chua co tren may, bam "Tai Voice" de tai ve (can mang lan dau).
     Sau khi tai, giong doc chay hoan toan offline, khong gui ra ngoai.
   - Chon "Long Tieng Vao Video" de xuat luon tep *_long_tieng.mp4
     hoac "Chi Xuat Tep Tieng" (WAV).
   - "Giu Tieng Goc" tu lay audio cua video va ha xuong 20% mac dinh.
   - Chon che do canh thoi gian:
     + "Canh theo sub": giu nguyen thoi gian video goc, cau doc bat dau
       dung moc sub, toc do chung giu co dinh (khong tu co gian tung cau,
       co the chong tieng neu bat cho phep chong).
     + "Canh theo giong doc": keo dai timeline video theo do dai phat am
       thuc te cua tung cau voice.
   - Co the chinh toc do, Bass/Mid/Treble, khoang nghi cuoi,
     luu tung voice va gan profile giong Mac dinh/Nam/Nu.
   - Bam "START: Long Tieng".
6. Tab "B4: Render va xuat" -> Render video

Muon chay lien mot mach: tich chon cac buoc o cot "Cac buoc chay tu dong"
goc duoi ben trai, roi bam "Chay kich ban".

Tai tab "Cau Hinh Chung", nhap ten va bam "Tao Moi" de luu mot bo cau hinh.
Chon ten trong danh sach de ap dung; nut "Xoa" xoa profile, khong xoa tep tam.
Nut "AI Gateway" o tab nay dung de cau hinh ket noi dich AI.

CAP NHAT PHAN MEM (UPDATER)
---------------------------
Vao tab "Cau Hinh Chung" -> bam "Kiem Tra Cap Nhat".
Neu co phien ban moi tren GitHub, bam "Cap Nhat Ngay". Trinh ho tro
AutoSubUpdater.exe se tu dong tai ban moi ve, thay the nguyen tu va khoi

GIONG DOC OFFLINE (PIPER TTS)
-----------------------------
Ban dong goi da co san runtime Piper trong thu muc _internal\piper\.
Cac model giong doc sau khi bam "Tai Voice" o tab B3 se duoc luu vao
Data\models\piper\ de dung offline vinh vien ma khong can mang.
Neu chay tu ma nguon: tai ban release Windows tu
https://github.com/rhasspy/piper/releases va giai nen vao assets\piper\
(can piper.exe va thu muc espeak-ng-data).


DU LIEU LUU O DAU
-----------------
Tat ca nam trong thu muc "Data" ngay canh tep chay:
    Data\workspace\projects\   -> du an, video, phu de, ket qua render
    Data\workspace\db\         -> danh sach du an
    Data\config\               -> cai dat va khoa API
    Data\models\asr\           -> mo hinh nhan dang giong noi (Whisper)
    Data\models\piper\         -> mo hinh giong doc offline (Piper)

Neu chep phan mem vao noi khong ghi duoc (vi du o dia CD), phan mem
tu chuyen sang luu o Documents va AppData nhu binh thuong.

LOI THUONG GAP
--------------
Mo len bao thieu FFmpeg
    -> Nhap dup KIEM-TRA-MAY-NAY.bat de xem thieu gi.
       Thu muc _internal\ffmpeg phai co ffmpeg.exe va ffprobe.exe.
       Neu thieu, ban da chep thieu tep khi mang folder sang may khac.

Nhan dang ra chu sai nhieu
    -> Chay buoc "Xoa nhac nen, giu loi thoai" truoc khi nhan dang.

Khong thay tep .srt canh video
    -> Xem o Nhat ky ben phai, dong "Da tu dong luu phu de: ..." se ghi
       ro duong dan. Neu thu muc video khong ghi duoc (o USB da rut,
       thu muc chi doc) thi tep nam trong thu muc "exports" cua du an.
       Cung kiem tra tab "Cai dat chung" xem o "Tach sub xong thi luu
       luon tep .srt canh video" con duoc tich khong.

Render rat lau
    -> Kiem tra xem may co dung card do hoa khong (KIEM-TRA-MAY-NAY.bat).
       Neu khong co card, tab B4 doi "Toc do ma hoa" sang veryfast
       va tang CRF len 24-26.

Tep render ra nang hon truoc
    -> Render bang card do hoa cho tep nang hon mot chut o cung muc
       chat luong. Tang CRF o tab B4 len 24-26 la tep nho lai.

Phan mem tu tat
    -> Xem tep Data\config\logs\crash.log
